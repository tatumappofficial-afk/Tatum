Privacy Policy

Tatum App LLC
Effective Date: July 24, 2026  ·  Pennsylvania

"Your intimate life belongs to you and nobody else. This is not a marketing statement — it is the architectural foundation of everything Tatum was built to be."

We will never sell your data. We will never share your data. We will never use your personal information for advertising. Your truth is yours.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tatum mobile application developed and operated by Tatum App LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. When this policy refers to "we," "us," or "our," it means Tatum App LLC. Contact us at tatum.app.official@gmail.com.

2. The Most Important Thing You Need To Know

All data you enter into Tatum — your session logs, partner information, activity tags, notes, satisfaction ratings, and any other personal information — is stored exclusively on your device. It never leaves your phone. We cannot see it, access it, read it, or retrieve it. Ever.

This is a technical fact built into the architecture of the App. Tatum operates without a server-side database of user wellness data. Your intimate life stays on your device and nowhere else.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Tatum is designed as a private, on-device application, we do not collect the following:

  • Your phone number or government-issued identification
  • Your session logs, partner profiles, activity tags, notes, or any intimate wellness data
  • Your location data
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Any biometric data used for Face ID or Touch ID — this is processed entirely by your device's operating system
  • Your browsing history or activity outside the App

4. Information We May Collect

4.1 Anonymous App Analytics

The App may use analytics tools to collect anonymous, non-personally identifiable information including crash reports, general feature usage statistics, and device type for compatibility purposes. This information is collected in aggregate form only, cannot be used to identify you personally, and is never linked to your intimate wellness data.

4.2 Purchase and Payment Information

Tatum is a one-time purchase of $24.99 processed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number, billing address, or any other payment information.

4.3 Account Information: Name and Email

When you create a Tatum account, we collect your name and email address. This is the only information that ever leaves your device. Your name and email are used to create and manage your account, to confirm your one-time purchase, and to send you occasional account-related communications. We do not use your name or email for advertising, marketing campaigns, or newsletters without your explicit consent, and we never sell, share, or transfer this information to any third party.

No other information — including your session logs, partner information, activity tags, notes, satisfaction ratings, or any other wellness data you enter into the App — is ever collected, transmitted, or stored anywhere other than your own device.

4.4 Customer Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we will receive and store the contents of your message and your email address solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not use support communications for marketing and do not share them with third parties.

5. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Creating and managing your Tatum account
  • Identifying and fixing technical bugs and crashes in the App
  • Understanding which features are most valuable to improve them
  • Confirming your one-time App purchase and providing access
  • Sending you occasional account-related communications
  • Responding to customer support inquiries

We do not use any information for advertising, marketing profiling, data brokering, or any purpose beyond those listed above.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for any commercial purpose. Period.

We may disclose information only if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or in the event of a business acquisition where we will notify you of any such change.

7. Data Security

7.1 On-Device Security

Your intimate wellness data is stored on your device using secure, encrypted on-device storage. The App uses your device's built-in security infrastructure to protect your data from unauthorized access.

7.2 Biometric and Passcode Protection

The App offers optional Face ID, Touch ID, and passcode lock features. We strongly encourage all users to enable these security features.

7.3 No Transmission, No Breach Risk

Because your intimate wellness data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers, it cannot be exposed in a server-side data breach. The most secure data is data that never travels.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your wellness data is stored on your device for as long as you have the App installed. You have complete control at all times:

  • Delete individual session logs at any time within the App
  • Export your data through the Settings screen before deletion
  • Deleting the App from your device permanently deletes all locally stored data

8.1 Delete Account & Data (In-App)

You can permanently delete your Tatum account at any time by opening the App and selecting Settings → Delete Account & Data. This immediately and permanently deletes your name and email address from our records, along with all wellness data stored on your device. This action cannot be undone.

8.2 Request Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have the App installed but want your name and email address deleted from our records, you can request deletion at any time by emailing us at tatum.app.official@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account." We will confirm your identity using the email address on file and delete your name and email within 7 business days. Because your wellness data (session logs, tags, notes) is never transmitted to us, there is no server-side wellness data to delete — only your account information (name and email) is held by us and subject to this request.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe your child has used the App, please contact us immediately.

10. Sensitive Personal Information

Tatum is designed to store intimate wellness data, which is considered a special category of sensitive personal information. Our approach is simple: we do not collect it, we do not store it on our servers, we do not transmit it, and we have no ability to access it. Your intimate wellness information — including any name or profile information you enter — exists solely on your device and is fully under your control at all times.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Right to Access

Because all your personal wellness data is stored on your own device, you have complete and immediate access to all of it at all times through the App.

Right to Delete

You have the right to delete your data at any time — individual entries, all wellness data on your device, or your full account (name and email) using Settings → Delete Account & Data within the App. If you no longer have the App installed, see Section 8.2 for how to request account deletion by email.

Right to Export

All Tatum users have the right to export their data in a portable format through the Settings screen.

Right to Delete Your Account (Name and Email)

You may request deletion of your name and email address at any time, either in-app via Settings → Delete Account & Data, or by emailing tatum.app.official@gmail.com. We will delete your account information within 7 business days of a request made by email.

California Residents (CCPA)

Because we do not sell personal information and do not collect personally identifiable intimate wellness data, many CCPA provisions may not apply to your use of Tatum. For any CCPA-related inquiries please contact us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes we will notify you through the App and update the effective date. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
