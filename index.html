The Story Behind Tatum

It started as a notebook in a nightstand.

For a long time I approached intimacy the way a lot of women do — like something I owed. Something on the list. Something I did for my relationship, for my partner, but rarely for myself. And when you approach it that way, resentment builds quietly until it becomes the whole feeling.

Then I started keeping a notebook in my nightstand. Just dates and a simple initial — my own private shorthand. Not to prove anything to anyone. Just to see my own truth clearly for once.

When I looked back at those pages, something shifted. I had been showing up. I had been present. I had been enough. And slowly, as I started seeing my intimate life as something that belonged to me — not something I was giving away — everything changed.

That notebook became Tatum. Built for every woman who deserves to feel that shift.

What Tatum Does

Built to give you your confidence back.

Every feature in Tatum was designed around one question — does this help her see that she is enough? That her intimate life belongs to her? That she deserves to want this for herself?

📅
Calendar View
Open Tatum and see your month clearly. Not to judge yourself — but to know yourself. Every date marked in your own private shorthand, for your eyes only.
✏️
Quick Log
Tap a date, tap an emoji, save. Logging takes under 30 seconds. The faster it is, the more consistently you do it — and the clearer your story becomes.
👤
Partner Profiles
Create profiles using only an initial or emoji. See patterns by partner. Your data, your language, nobody else's labels.
📊
Insights Dashboard
Look back at a week, a month, a year and watch your confidence build. When you can see your own patterns clearly, the self-doubt starts to dissolve.
😊
Emotional Tags
Log how it felt — not just that it happened. Passionate, playful, tender, present, or just yours. Your emotional experience matters and Tatum is the first tracker that knows that.
📖
Private Journal
Add a note to any entry. A sentence, a feeling, a memory. Only you ever read it. Your most private thoughts, kept exactly where they belong.
Privacy First. Always.

Your intimate life belongs to you.

Your data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Nobody can. Ever.

On-Device Only
Everything you log stays on your phone. No servers. No cloud backup. No database of any kind.
👤
No Account Required
No email. No username. No registration. Tatum does not know who you are and that is exactly the point.
🤫
Biometric Lock
Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode protects the app every time it opens. Anyone who picks up your phone cannot get in.

No Data Sales. Ever.
We will never sell your data. Never share it. Never use it for advertising. Your truth is yours. Full stop.

Discreet Icon
The app icon is a simple bloom mark. Nothing about it announces what the app is. Your privacy starts before you open it.

No Breach Risk
Data that never travels cannot be exposed. Because your data never leaves your device it cannot be intercepted.
